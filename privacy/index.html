Tiny Skies — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Tiny Skies ("the game") is made by William Carrion ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What the game stores on your device

Your airline progress (birds, coins, wings, airports and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run a server that receives your game data. If you use the backup code feature, the code stays with you.

Pilot name and gameplay analytics

When you start, the game asks what to call you. This name is shown in the game and stays on your device. Any nickname works. On Android, the game may use Google Analytics for Firebase to see how the game is played: for example when you level up, open an airport, finish the tutorial, or how long you play. These events are linked only to a random ID created by the game, and never include your pilot name, contacts, exact location or payment details. The iPhone version does not use Firebase. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device information and how you interact with ads. In the EEA, the UK and other regions where it's required, you are asked for consent through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game. On iPhone you are asked for permission before your advertising identifier is used for tracking. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store. We use RevenueCat to check purchases and restore them on a new device. RevenueCat receives purchase receipts and an anonymous app user ID. We never receive your payment details. RevenueCat's policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings. You can delete all game data by uninstalling the game or by using Settings → Reset all progress.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game: tinyskieshelp@icloud.com
