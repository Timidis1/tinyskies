Got a new phone?

Anything you bought comes back: open the Wings tab and tap Restore purchases at the bottom. To move your airline itself, use Settings → Backup on the old phone to copy a backup code, then Restore from code on the new one.

Ads

You can change how ads may use your data any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. The Remove Ads pack turns off the video breaks between flights.

Refunds

Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play, so refunds are requested there: on iPhone at reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history.
